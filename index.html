Key Features & Hidden Shortcuts
  • Resync Beat 1 (Downbeat)
    Tap the first circle directly under the BPM counter (or the central pulse flasher) at any time to instantly resynchronize to the 1st beat on the fly without stopping the metronome.
  • Concert Flame: Candle or Bic Lighter
    In Gadgets, choose between a romantic Candle or an iconic Bic Lighter (complete with human thumb pressing the gas valve). You can also switch between them directly on stage with the quick toggle button.
  • Songs with Unknown BPM ("-")
    In Setlist mode, songs can be saved without a BPM (using -). Clicking Play on them selects the song and time signature, switches to Metronome mode without auto-starting, and displays - so you can choose or tap your tempo.
  • Quick BPM Presets [60, 90, 120, 140, 180]
    Presets are ordered from slowest to fastest. You can reorder buttons with drag & drop, or use Save Preset to store any current tempo onto a preset button.
  • Auto-BPM Microphone Detection
    Detects tempo automatically from live acoustic music. If the metronome is currently playing when you open Auto-BPM, a prompt allows you to stop it with 1 tap for clean listening.
  • Free Mode to Setlist Shortcut
    Tap the top banner area (marked Free Mode) to jump directly to the Setlists and Songs screen.
  • Reload Latest Version
    The bottom footer button completely clears offline service worker caches and fetches the latest GitHub release immediately, with no need to delete browser cookies or site data.
